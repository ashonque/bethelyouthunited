Who We Are

About Us

Bethel Youth United (BYU), under the Bethel International Pentecostal Church (B.I.P.C.), Germany, is led by Brother Sharon Shaju.

At Bethel Youth United, we are passionate about loving God and loving people, because love is the most important foundation of the Christian faith. Jesus said, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind. This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: Love your neighbor as yourself." (Matthew 22:37-39)

What began in 2022 as simple Zoom meetings has grown powerfully—hosting two impactful conferences and uniting young people from diverse backgrounds, nationalities, languages, and ethnicities, all seeking a genuine encounter with God.

Our worship flows in multiple languages: Malayalam, English, Tamil, German, Hindi, and more—reflecting the multicultural heartbeat of our church family.

The revival among our youth is truly miraculous. Young lives are being transformed—experiencing healing, water baptism in the name of Jesus, and receiving the baptism of the Holy Ghost with the evidence of speaking in tongues. Our meetings are filled with authentic freedom in worship.

We offer dynamic weekly fellowships, powerful worship sessions, Bible studies, prayer meetings, youth conferences, camps, mentorship, and opportunities to serve and grow in leadership.

By God's grace, Bethel Youth United extends beyond Germany, connecting with branch churches in Germany, Sweden, Lithuania, and across Europe.

"You will feel a move of God, and it's a family!"

We warmly invite you—whether you're a teen, young adult, or youth at heart—to come and experience the fresh fire of Pentecost. Experience His Glory. Enter His Presence. Receive His Love! 🔥
