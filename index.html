Who We Are

About Us

Bethel Youth United (BYU), under the Bethel International Pentecostal Church (B.I.P.C.), Germany, is led by Brother Sharon Shaju.

What began in 2022 as simple Zoom meetings has grown powerfully—hosting two impactful conferences and uniting young people from diverse backgrounds, nationalities, languages, and ethnicities, all seeking a genuine encounter with God.

Our Mission

We are passionate about loving God and loving people. It is the foundation of our faith. "Love the Lord your God with all your heart... and your neighbor as yourself." (Matthew 22:37-39)

Indian Christian Community

Our worship flows in multiple languages: Malayalam, English, Tamil, German, Hindi, and more — reflecting our Kerala Pentecostal heritage and Indian Christian traditions in Germany.

Revival & Growth

Young lives are being transformed daily — experiencing healing, water baptism, and the baptism of the Holy Ghost. Our connection stretches across Germany, Sweden, Lithuania, and Europe.

"You will feel a move of God, and it's a family!"

We offer dynamic weekly fellowships, worship services, Bible studies, prayer meetings, youth conferences, camps, and mentorships. We warmly invite you — whether you're looking for a Bethel Pentecostal church in Germany or just exploring faith — to come and experience the fresh fire of Pentecost.

Experience His Glory. Enter His Presence. Receive His Love! 🔥
